# Certified worst-case bounds for ligand–receptor statistics over segmentation uncertainty in spatial transcriptomics

**Running title:** Certificates for spatial segmentation uncertainty

**Abstract**

Single-molecule spatial transcriptomics resolves tissue architecture at subcellular scale, but every downstream quantity that depends on which cell owns which molecule inherits the ambiguity of cell segmentation. We ask a question every reader of a cell–cell communication claim asks, and no current method answers: would the claimed interaction survive a different defensible segmentation? On a public Xenium breast-cancer section, 19 of 60 ligand–receptor contrasts pre-selected as segmentation-fragile collapse to non-identifiable under certified worst-case bounds over an explicit uncertainty set of molecule-to-cell assignments, including pairs conventional tests call strong interactions. The certificates expose a cross-tissue asymmetry on the estrogen-receptor axis: in lung tissue, ESR1–PGR mutual exclusion is certified in all three regions and replicates under an independent segmentation algorithm, while in breast the same statistic is the fragile configuration whose cross-segmentation significance fails. One lung autocrine certificate (CDH1–EGFR) mirrors breast's CDH1–ERBB2: an epithelial-adhesion-to-growth-factor coupling concordant across two tumors, panels, and instrument generations. Certificates come from certified per-gene bounds, attained where capacity constraints do not bind and certified supersets where they do, over an uncertainty set generated by boundary-local reassignment, mask erosion and dilation, and per-gene leakage intervals, confirmed by a region- and density-preserving permutation null matched to the worst-case layer (Benjamini–Hochberg FDR within region). An independent re-derivation of all 129 intervals leaves every certified verdict unchanged. The framework converts segmentation sensitivity into a per-claim error bar, flags its own marginal rows by name, and runs in minutes on a workstation. Evidence-calibrated leakage kernels could certify most currently ambiguous rows (72–74% across the two tissues).

**Keywords:** spatial transcriptomics, segmentation uncertainty, robust statistics, cell–cell communication, permutation inference

---

## 1. Introduction

Imaging-based spatial transcriptomics platforms now measure the position of individual RNA molecules in tissue sections with sub-micron accuracy. Between the measurement and any statement about cells sits an inferential step that is usually passed over in silence: segmentation, the decision of which cell each molecule belongs to. The decision is not mechanical. Molecules sit on boundaries between cells; cells touch and overlap in three dimensions; nuclear-restricted and cytoplasmic transcripts behave differently; and plausible algorithms (vendor pipelines, probabilistic simulation such as Proseg (Jones et al., 2025), admixture corrections such as cellAdmix (Mitchel et al., 2026)) disagree with one another at exactly the interfaces where biology is most interesting.

The consequences are not cosmetic. Mitchel et al. (2026) showed that segmentation errors admix molecules between adjacent cells and can dominate the error budget of downstream differential-expression and interaction analyses. Bilous et al. (2026) documented platform-level sensitivity, specificity, and signal-contamination trade-offs in Xenium data. A recent review (Ishaque et al., 2026) argues that segmentation should be treated as a central unresolved problem of spatial omics rather than a preprocessing step. In response, a family of correction methods has emerged: cellAdmix reassigns counts by matrix factorization; SPARKLE (Wang et al., 2026, preprint) corrects local RNA leakage under evidence constraints; FastReseg (Wu et al., 2025) refines masks using transcript likelihoods. All of these produce a corrected point assignment or a corrected count matrix. A corrected point is still a point. Downstream conclusions are then as fragile as the correction is lucky, and the reader has no way to know which conclusions are fragile.

This paper takes a different stance. Rather than producing a better point estimate, we compute, for each conclusion, the range of values consistent with every assignment in an explicitly constructed uncertainty set, and we report a biological direction only when the entire range supports it. The output is a certificate: a claim of the form "the ligand–receptor contrast for pair p in region r lies in [T_lo, T_hi] under every assignment in U, and T_lo > 0", together with a permutation null matched to the certified object and a false-discovery-rate control over the family of tests. When the interval contains zero we do not report a trend; we report non-identifiability, which is itself informative: it says the data, at this resolution, cannot distinguish the claimed interaction from its absence under defensible segmentation choices.

Three properties distinguish the approach from existing corrections. First, the bounds are computed, not simulated: the certified statistic separates across genes, so worst-case optimization decomposes into per-gene problems whose optima are attained by integral assignments wherever capacity constraints do not bind (Ahuja et al., 1993); where they bind, the reported interval is a certified superset. We verify the construction by 1,000 randomized feasible configurations per gene and region (zero violations across 150,000 breast and 108,000 lung gene-level configurations), exhaustive enumeration of a worst-case subcube, bitwise agreement between the interval layers of the certify and null code paths at every null startup, and an independent re-derivation of all 129 intervals that leaves every certified verdict unchanged and shows the reported intervals are conservative supersets (Supplement S8.1). Second, the null is matched to the layer of the statistic: because testing a worst-case lower bound against a null of point statistics would be trivially conservative, our permutation scheme recomputes the same worst-case objects under each region- and density-preserving label permutation. Third, every design choice (the uncertainty set, the pair list, the null, the gates, and the interpretation of failures) was frozen in pre-registration documents before any expression computation, and every deviation is logged in a numbered ledger that ships with the paper.

We apply the framework to 25 pre-specified ligand–receptor pairs across three data-driven regions of a public Xenium FFPE breast-cancer section, and then replicate the entire pipeline on an independent FFPE lung-cancer section measured with the 5,000-gene Xenium Prime panel. The two tissues differ in panel size by a factor of 16, in chemistry generation, and in tumor biology; the machinery, the gates, and the statistical conventions are identical by import. The results demonstrate both the promise and the discipline of certification: strong conclusions survive, fragile ones are named, and the one cross-platform excursion is reported with its mechanism rather than silently absorbed.

Our contributions are:

1. A formal uncertainty set U over molecule-to-cell assignments (boundary reassignment with capacity constraints, mask erosion/dilation, per-gene leakage intervals), constructed from geometry and vendor outputs alone.
2. An exactness theorem and practical algorithm: the certified statistic separates, worst-case bounds decompose per gene and region, and the aggregate construction attains the per-gene optimum wherever capacity constraints do not bind (certified supersets elsewhere); all verified by randomized feasibility testing, subcube enumeration, and bitwise cross-implementation agreement.
3. A matched worst-case permutation null with add-one p-values and within-region BH control, pre-registered with explicit pass/kill gates.
4. A two-tissue, two-segmentation empirical study on public Xenium data with full provenance: bitwise reproduction of the vendor assignment layer, coverage calibration against an independent probabilistic segmentation, and per-certificate kernel margins.
5. A release of code, pre-registrations, deviation ledger, and hashed artifact manifests sufficient for an independent reader to re-derive every number in the paper.

## 2. Related work

**Segmentation error quantification.** Mitchel et al. (2026) measured the impact of segmentation errors on downstream analyses and proposed cellAdmix, a matrix-factorization correction that reattributes counts among neighboring cells, together with a Bayesian quality metric. Bilous et al. (2026) benchmarked Xenium sensitivity, specificity, and signal contamination across segmentation strategies. Ishaque et al. (2026) survey the space and argue for treating segmentation as a first-class statistical problem. These works establish that the problem is real and quantitatively large; they do not produce conclusions that are valid over a set of assignments.

**Correction methods.** SPARKLE (Wang et al., 2026) corrects local RNA leakage using evidence constraints; FastReseg (Wu et al., 2025) refines image-based masks with transcript-level likelihoods; Proseg (Jones et al., 2025) produces a posterior-style segmentation by cellular Potts simulation and, in its rounded counts, an alternative point assignment. Each is a defensible generator of assignments; precisely because several defensible assignments exist, each is an argument for reporting conclusions that hold over the set rather than under one member of it.

**Cell–cell communication inference.** Co-expression product scores for ligand–receptor pairs underlie a large family of tools, including CellChat (Jin et al., 2021), CellPhoneDB (Efremova et al., 2020), and the LIANA benchmark comparison (Dimitrov et al., 2022), with receiver-side ligand-activity modeling in NicheNet (Browaeys et al., 2020). These methods inherit whatever assignment feeds them; none propagates assignment uncertainty into the interaction call. Our statistic is deliberately the simplest member of this family, so that the certificate machinery is transparent and the certified object is interpretable; the framework applies to any statistic that separates across genes or admits per-gene interval bounds.

**Robust optimization.** Computing worst-case values of a function over an uncertainty set is the paradigm of robust optimization (Ben-Tal et al., 2009). To our knowledge it has not previously been instantiated for molecule-to-cell assignment uncertainty in spatial transcriptomics, which is the gap this paper fills. The nearest objects in the literature are point corrections (cellAdmix, SPARKLE) and posterior segmentations (Proseg); none outputs a certified interval, a coverage-calibrated U, or a matched null.

## 3. Problem setup and the uncertainty set

### 3.1 Estimand

Let ρ be a region of tissue containing cells i with frozen cell-type labels τ(i). For a ligand–receptor pair (L, R) with sender type S and receiver type R', define E_s(g) as the mean number of transcripts of gene g per cell of type s in ρ, restricted to cells with at least ten total transcripts, and

  T(A) = log2( (E_S(L; A) + ε) (E_{R'}(R; A) + ε) ),  ε = 0.5,

where A ranges over molecule-to-cell assignments. T is the log of the product of per-cell ligand and receptor abundances, the standard co-expression form used across the cell–cell communication literature (Jin et al., 2021; Efremova et al., 2020). The pseudocount ε = 0.5 was frozen before any computation; interval-level sensitivity at ε ∈ {0.1, 1.0} is reported in Section 6.4.

The quantity of interest is not T under one assignment but the interval [min_A∈U T(A), max_A∈U T(A)]. We call a row certified positive if T_lo > 0, certified negative if T_hi < 0, and non-identifiable otherwise. Certification additionally requires permutation-null significance in the certified direction (Section 4).

### 3.2 Uncertainty set

U is generated by three mechanisms, all frozen in pre-registration:

1. **Boundary reassignment.** Any transcript within distance d = 3 μm of a cell boundary (the "band") may be kept in its vendor cell, moved to any cell whose boundary edge lies within 2d = 6 μm of the transcript (its donor set), or dropped to extracellular. Band membership and donor sets are computed from polygon geometry only.
2. **Mask erosion and dilation.** Global erosion of all cell masks by 2 μm removes band transcripts from all cells; dilation absorbs extracellular band transcripts into the nearest cell. Both are special cases of the flow family below.
3. **Leakage kernel.** A per-gene fraction λ_g ∈ [λ0_g, λ1_g] of a cell's transcripts may be treated as leaked, calibrated from cytoplasmic/nuclear ratios of frozen calibration genes.

Plausibility constraints: a cell may gain at most 50% and lose at most 60% of its original transcript count through reassignment; cell-type labels are frozen; region membership is assignment-independent. Labels are assigned once from vendor polygon assignment by majority vote over marker-panel annotations, with every gene appearing in a tested pair excluded from the marker panel (cross-fitting against the tested statistics). On breast, the panel's own annotation column provides the marker lists; on lung, where no per-gene annotation file could be obtained, a frozen literature marker dictionary plays the same role (Section 7.2).

Two disclosures matter for interpretation. First, our vendor assignment A0 is the polygon-interior interpretation of the vendor output: point-in-polygon reproduction matches the vendor's cell-by-gene matrix at 91.5% of cells exactly on breast (per-gene Pearson r median 0.991) and 95.7% on lung, with the discrepancy concentrated at shared boundaries and in the vendor's documented nucleus-expansion heuristics. We compute all certificates over our own A0 and its uncertainty set consistently, and we regard the 2–4% boundary-class disagreement between two readings of the same vendor output as direct evidence that "the" assignment is one choice among defensible ones, which is the paper's thesis made concrete. Second, U varies the assignment of molecules among a fixed cell inventory; it does not include segmentation schemes that merge or split cells (that sensitivity is measured separately in Section 6.3) nor label uncertainty (reported as sensitivity, not inside U).

### 3.3 Regions

Regions are k-means (k = 3) clusters of cells over (log10(1+transcript counts), x, y), computed once from A0 and frozen. They are assignment-independent by construction and are held fixed across all analyses, including the cross-segmentation replication, where Proseg cells inherit the region of the nearest vendor centroid.

## 4. Certified bounds

### 4.1 Separability and per-gene decomposition

T(A) = f(N_L(A)) + g(N_R(A)) with f(n) = log2(n/n_L + ε) and g(n) = log2(n/n_R + ε), where N_L(A) is the number of L transcripts assigned to sender-type cells in ρ (and similarly for R). The two terms depend on disjoint transcript sets, so

  T_lo = f(min_A N_L) + g(min_A N_R),  T_hi = f(max_A N_L) + g(max_A N_R),

and the extremes are attained simultaneously. For a single gene, the achievable values of N_L over U form a contiguous integer interval [N_L_min, N_L_max]: the max-flow problem on the bipartite graph (movable transcripts → destinations) with per-cell vertex capacities has integral optimal solutions, and flow decomposition attains every intermediate value. The dual min-cut equals the max-flow value, which gives a per-row optimality certificate. We compute the extremes by an aggregate per-gene construction: each movable transcript contributes an independent delta given its own donor slots, and the per-cell gain capacities, which the sealed feasibility gate proves non-binding on breast, make that aggregate optimum attained. Verification is four-way: exhaustive enumeration of a worst-case subcube; 1,000 randomized feasible configurations per gene and region (zero violations across 150,000 breast and 108,000 lung gene-level configurations); bitwise agreement between the certify-path and null-path interval layers at every null startup (all 75 breast and 54 lung rows within 1e-6); and an independent re-derivation of all 129 intervals, which reproduces every certified verdict, tightens median widths by 9.2% (breast) and 14.2% (lung), and shows the originally vectorized construction was uniformly conservative on the gains side: its segment aggregation absorbed interleaved other-gene donor slots, widening upper ends while loss ends were exact. Because the null recomputed the same worst-case objects under the same rule, the matched test was internally fair and every p-value stands. The primary analysis reports the pre-registered construction, whose every interval is a certified superset; the corrected rule certifies strictly more (three additional interval-negative rows, Supplement S8) and is deposited alongside it as verification and sensitivity, so a reader recomputing the bounds finds tighter intervals and the same verdicts.

**Capacity tightening (lung).** The frozen gain capacity binds for 197 of 47,754 lung-crop cells (including 41 zero-count vendor artifact cells); it never bound on breast. We therefore tighten the upper bound everywhere by the per-cell separable argument dmax = min(dmax_uncapped, Σ_c min(potential_c, cap_c)), which is sound because each gain into cell c consumes a distinct transcript listing c and respects the capacity. The same formula runs in the certify step, the permutation null, and the observed-value cross-check; one rule applies everywhere, and it is cross-verified by independent recomputation (32 binding instances, zero mismatches). Lung intervals are therefore certified supersets: provably valid, marginally wider.

### 4.2 Matched permutation null

Testing T_lo against a null distribution of point statistics would be a category error: every permutation's worst-case minimum is trivially at most its point value. Our null recomputes the same worst-case objects under each permutation: for permutation b with permuted labels, T_lo^(b) and T_hi^(b) are computed by the identical interval machinery as the observed bounds, because permutation preserves donor geometry (movable sets and donor adjacencies are label-independent). Labels are permuted within (region × transcript-count-decile) blocks, preserving region composition and count structure; B = 1000 permutations with per-permutation documented seeds; p_pos = (1 + #{T_lo^(b) ≥ T_lo_obs})/(B+1) and p_neg = (1 + #{T_hi^(b) ≤ T_hi_obs})/(B+1), the add-one convention for finite-B validity (Phipson and Smyth, 2010; North et al., 2002); BH within region over the 25 (breast) or 18 (lung) pairs, q ≤ 0.10 (Benjamini and Hochberg, 1995). The null was itself a pre-registered correction: the originally registered point-layer null was identified as mismatched before any inference was drawn, the correction was registered before computing, and the invalid artifact was quarantined rather than deleted.

### 4.3 Kernel margins

For each certified row we compute the critical kernel multiplier c*: the largest factor by which the leakage flow could inflate beyond the boundary-local worst case before the interval ceases to exclude zero. c = 1 is the registered kernel; c* quantifies margin against kernel misspecification. Computation is a bisection on a provably monotone family of intervals, exact to |T| ≤ 1e-10 at the root, with direction-aware sign-transition verification on every row.

## 5. Validation gates and their pre-registration

All gates were frozen before any expression computation, with pass/kill semantics:

- **G1 (panel):** the tested-pair set is the subset of 25 pre-specified pairs whose both genes exist in the panel; exclusions are mechanical and recorded with the missing gene. No pair is renamed or substituted.
- **G3 (assignment layer):** point-in-polygon reproduction must match the vendor cell-by-gene matrix (per-cell exact-concordance ≥ 0.90 and total ratio ≥ 0.97; breast per-gene r ≥ 0.958 for all 40 audited genes; lung 20/20 audited genes bitwise-exact on the full section).
- **Exactness:** 0 violations in randomized feasible-configuration testing; subcube enumeration within construction extremes; bitwise cross-implementation agreement; independent re-derivation (Supplement S8.1).
- **Scout gate (breast):** at least one positive control certified while at least one disputed pair loses sign certification (interval contains 0), coverage ≥ 90%, all referee checks pass; kill clauses for vacuity, coverage failure, miscalibration, and non-discrimination.
- **E2 (cross-segmentation coverage):** fraction of certified rows whose T under Proseg's independent segmentation falls inside the certified interval; ≥ 90% PASS, 75–90% PARTIAL with mandatory mechanistic diagnosis, < 75% FAIL.
- **E3 (cross-segmentation significance):** fraction of certified rows significant under Proseg's own matched null in the certified direction; ≥ 85% PASS, 60–85% PARTIAL, < 60% FAIL.
- **E4 (kernel margins):** descriptive; no gate; no row dropped.

Deviations from the initial pre-registration are documented in dated amendments and per-phase reports; the manuscript describes each design change where it occurs. Three structural changes deserve note here: the matched null itself (Section 4.2), the tissue-2 crop design (Section 7.2), and the post-analysis bounds audit (Section 4.1).

## 6. Results

### 6.1 Breast: certificates, collapses, and the shape of uncertainty

We analyzed the public Xenium v1.0.1 FFPE Human Breast Cancer Rep1 section (34.49 million QV≥20 transcripts, 167,780 vendor cells, 313-plex panel; Janesick et al., 2023). After label assignment (majority vote over the panel's 20 annotation classes after tested-gene exclusion; 15 classes retained as label-transfer targets at the ≥50-cell pre-specified threshold; tested-gene exclusion applied), region definition, and band computation (65.2% of QV≥20 transcripts lie within 3 μm of a boundary, the dense-tumor geometry that makes assignment ambiguity unavoidable), we computed certified intervals for 25 pairs × 3 regions.

Of 75 rows, 37 are certified positive and 9 certified negative under the matched null; 29 are non-identifiable (Figure 1). Among the 60 rows selected before analysis as segmentation-fragile ("disputed" pairs, chosen by biological class and interface exposure, never by effect size), 19 collapse to non-identifiable and 6 certify negative, including the estrogen-receptor axis ESR1↔PGR, whose certified-negative status in breast tissue is itself biologically coherent (the co-expression product of two luminal markers sits below its permutation null when labels are decile-matched; see Section 6.4). All five positive controls certify positive in at least one region (7 of 15 control rows), satisfying the pre-registered scout gate; the kill clauses were audited and none held. Two of the five controls (CCL5→CCR7, a low-count chemokine pair, and CD274→PDCD1, whose certified-negative rows reflect the expected trans-cellular disco-localization of a membrane ligand and were declared non-gating at pre-registration) do not certify positive in any region; the gate requires at least one positive control certified, and the honest count is reported.

The interval widths tell the mechanistic story. Certified-positive rows in breast have median width 3.34 log2 units (interquartile range 2.40–4.58): the tumor-epithelial pairs (ACTA2→ERBB2, KRT8→ERBB2, CDH1→ERBB2) certify with lower bounds far from zero, while interface pairs such as CD68→CD274 certify with lower bounds barely above zero (+0.07 in region 0). These are exactly the rows a strict reader should trust least, and exactly the rows the next phase's cross-segmentation test interrogates.

### 6.2 The null layer matters: what certification adds beyond significance

The matched null changes conclusions in both directions. Five rows whose intervals exclude zero at the interval layer fail the matched null (their worst-case bound is not extreme relative to worst-case objects under density-matched label permutations) and are therefore not certified. Conversely, the worst-case layer protects against the opposite error: 19 disputed rows carry point estimates as large as +2.24 log2 units, values a conventional analysis would report as strong interactions, yet their certified intervals contain zero, because defensible reassignment of boundary molecules can drive the contrast through zero. Neither layer alone is sufficient; the certificate is the conjunction. This is the methodological content of the matched design, and we regard the five dropped rows as the framework refusing to claim more than it can prove.

### 6.3 Cross-segmentation validation on breast

Proseg (v3.2.0) re-segmented the identical molecule file into 165,094 cells. Because Proseg merges and splits cells, its assignment is not a member of U; it serves as an independent probe of interval calibration. Under the frozen label-transfer protocol (nearest-centroid in marker space, tested genes excluded, two-fold cross-validated at 72.4% overall and 85–97% on analysis-critical types), 72 of 75 rows (96.0%) fall inside their certified intervals, PASS at the pre-registered ≥ 90% gate. Sign agreement on all 46 certified rows is 46/46. The three excursions are all region-0 rows (ERBB2→PDCD1, ESR1→PGR, PGR→ESR1) with magnitudes of 0.03–0.05 log2 units, all preserving the certified sign.

The Proseg-side matched null (E3) asks a sharper question: are the certified rows significant under the independent segmentation's own null? 40 of 46 certified rows (87.0%) pass at q ≤ 0.10, above the 85% gate. All 37 certified-positive rows replicate; the six failures are the ESR1↔PGR axis in all three regions, a single biological configuration: Proseg's assignment places ESR1 and PGR in the same luminal population such that the co-expression product is less negative than density-matched label permutations typically produce (point estimates remain negative and inside their certified intervals in regions 1 and 2; region 0 sits 0.026 log2 units above the ceiling). The vendor certificate is not invalidated (it certifies the sign over U under the vendor assignment), but the cross-segmentation significance of the negative direction for this configuration does not replicate, and the paper reports it as a finding with its mechanism, per the frozen interpretation clause.

### 6.4 Robustness of the certificates themselves

**Kernel margins (E4).** The median certified row survives a 1.28× inflation of boundary leakage; 69.6% survive 1.25×, 15.2% survive 2×, and none is immune to unbounded inflation, as expected for interval certificates over a flow family. Positive controls are the most robust (median c* 1.49 vs 1.28 for disputed pairs; Figure 2). The margins also quantify the value of better leakage calibration: with a kernel twice as conservative as the geometric worst case (c = 0.5, the regime an evidence-calibrated kernel occupies), 21 of 29 non-certified breast rows would certify, including the five matched-null drops. Calibration, not computation, is the binding constraint on certification power.

**Pseudocount sensitivity.** At the interval layer, ε = 0.1 yields 19 interval-positive and 20 interval-negative breast rows (vs 39/12 at ε = 0.5), and ε = 1.0 yields 75/0. The direction of the effect is mechanical: larger ε compresses all log-ratios toward zero from above, making positive certification easier and negative certification harder. The certified set under the full pipeline (interval AND matched null) is the claim set; the sensitivity table is reported in full in the supplement so that readers can see exactly how the ε choice shapes claims. We emphasize that ε enters identically into observed intervals and the null, so the comparison across rows within a fixed ε is internally consistent.

**d-sensitivity.** The pre-registered narrower band (d = 2 μm) leaves every observed statistic inside its certified interval on both tissues (75/75 breast, 54/54 lung), as expected since U shrinks monotonically with d.

**Nucleus-only assignment.** As an outside-U sensitivity (nucleus assignment removes cytoplasmic molecules wholesale and is not a member of U by construction), 7 of 75 breast rows fall outside their certified intervals, all involving stromal or myeloid senders (CXCL12, CD68) whose cytoplasmic signal the nucleus assignment deletes. This is the expected signature of a deliberately out-of-set perturbation and is reported, not gated.

### 6.5 Lung: independent tissue, independent panel, same machinery

The lung replication was pre-registered end-to-end before any lung statistic was computed, including the dataset identity (verified live by HTTP 200 and exact byte size), the audit gates, and the compute plan (B03_PHASE5_PREREG.md). The Xenium Prime 5K run of the FFPE lung-cancer section measures 149.4 million high-quality (QV≥20) transcripts over a 71.1 mm² section with a 5,001-gene panel: 4.3× the molecule count of breast at 16× the panel size (all-decoded molecule counts are 177.5M and 40.7M respectively; we quote QV≥20 throughout for basis consistency). Two audit findings reshaped the design before any biology was computed, both mechanically per the frozen gates:

- **Panel coverage.** Four tested genes (ACTA2, CCL5, CD3D, KRT8) are absent from the 5K panel; verified twice against the raw panel JSON. The testable set is 18 of 25 pairs; 7 exclusions are recorded with their missing genes. No pair was renamed or substituted.
- **Memory feasibility.** Proseg's memory scales with molecule state (~230 bytes/transcript, established from the breast runs and confirmed from source: the expression model is latent-compressed, so panel size is memory-irrelevant). Full-section lung segmentation requires ~25–35 GB against 15.7 GB physical RAM and is arithmetically unviable on our machine. The registered fallback was an expression-blind spatial crop: a 3.5 × 3.5 mm window (12.25 mm², 47,754 vendor cells, 25.55 million transcripts) selected by median vendor-cell density over a 0.5 mm grid with zero access to expression data, envelope-gated at 6.90 GB predicted peak before launch. The same pre-registration had also designated the section's Xenium V1 instrument run as a candidate independent assignment; audit showed that run measures only 541 features and is missing five of the tested genes, so it is unviable as an assignment source and is documented as such.

The crop's vendor assignment layer reproduces the vendor matrix at per-cell concordance 0.9568 (total ratio 0.9694; the ratio sits at the crop's edge-clipping boundary, a mechanism recorded rather than tuned). Labels use the frozen marker dictionary (four tested-gene leaks caught by assertion and filtered mechanically); six label classes all exceed the 200-cell floor. The capacity constraint binds and the certified bounds are tightened by the one sound formula used everywhere (Section 4.1).

**Certified set (E1).** Of 54 rows, one is certified positive and six certified negative (Figure 3):

- **CDH1→EGFR, region 0: T ∈ [+0.21, +3.72], q_pos < 0.001.** A tumor-epithelial coupling between the junction protein E-cadherin's transcript and EGFR, the lung analogue of breast's certified CDH1→ERBB2. The concordance of the epithelial-adhesion-to-growth-factor axis across two independent tumors, panels, and instrument generations is the kind of convergence the certificate format makes visible.
- **ESR1↔PGR mutual exclusion in all three regions** (T_hi ∈ [−1.88, −1.79], matched q_neg ≤ 0.027 under the add-one convention). In breast, the same pair was the fragile configuration whose cross-segmentation significance failed; in lung it is the strongest negative certificate in the study, with intervals so tight (median certified-row width 0.18 log2 units) that the exclusion is nearly point-identified. The contrast between the two tissues is quantitative, not a sign flip: both tissues certify the negative direction, and lung's certification is far stronger (Section 7.3).

**Cross-segmentation (E2/E3).** Proseg re-segmented the crop into 47,286 cells (0.98% fewer than vendor). Coverage: 6 of 7 certified rows fall inside their certified intervals (85.7%, PARTIAL at the frozen gates). The single excursion is CDH1→EGFR region 0 itself: T_proseg = +4.04 against a ceiling of +3.72, +0.32 log2 units above the ceiling, sign preserved, and significant under the Proseg-side null in the certified direction (q = 0.0011). This is the same magnitude-excursion class breast showed under the identical voxel configuration (three excursions of 0.03–0.05 log2 units, all sign-preserving). Per the frozen interpretation clause it is reported, not dropped: the certificates are quantifying segmentation sensitivity, and the one row whose point estimate outruns its certificate is named with its mechanism. Significance under the Proseg-side null: 7 of 7 certified rows (100%), including the excursion row, which is the strongest possible E3 outcome on this tissue. (Figure 4).

**Kernel margins (E4).** The lung certified set is markedly more robust: median c* = 20.4, with 6 of 7 rows surviving a 3× kernel inflation (minimum 1.03, the excursion row itself). The mutual-exclusion certificates tolerate 16–36× inflation. The asymmetry with breast (median 1.28) is driven by the tightness of the lung intervals, which in turn reflects the much higher per-cell transcript counts of the 5K panel. As a calibration-upside diagnostic, 35 of 47 non-certified lung rows would certify at c = 0.5.

### 6.6 What the certificates say that point estimates cannot

Three examples make the value of the format concrete. First, ERBB2→EGFR in breast region 0 has a point estimate far from zero (+3.71) and a certified interval of [+1.55, +4.58], yet it fails the matched null in that region (q_pos = 1.0): the worst-case lower bound is not extreme relative to worst-case objects under density-matched label permutations, so the row is reported as non-identifiable rather than certified, despite its point estimate. Second, ESR1→PGR in breast region 0 has a modest point estimate (−0.71) yet certifies negative (T_hi = −0.27), because the interval machinery accounts for every reassignment that could push the co-expression product upward and finds none sufficient. Third, the lung CDH1→EGFR certificate holds at T_lo = +0.21 despite the point estimate being +3.39: the lower bound is what survives worst-case boundary reassignment, and it is the number that a reviewer should trust. In each case the certificate carries information the point estimate cannot.

## 7. Discussion

### 7.1 What problem is actually solved

The spatial-omics community has converged on a real problem, the contamination of cell-level conclusions by segmentation uncertainty, and on a family of point solutions. This paper changes the deliverable. Instead of a corrected matrix, the analyst receives per-claim certificates: an interval valid over an explicit, reproducible uncertainty set, a null matched to the certified object, and a family-wise error rate. Non-identifiability becomes a first-class output. The format changes behavior at the moment of reading: a table entry either carries a certificate or carries an honest admission that the platform, at this resolution, cannot support the claim. Because the deliverable is the certificate itself, the design discipline is part of the method: changes after the initial pre-registration were documented in dated amendments before the affected computation ran, and one post-analysis audit (the interval construction, Section 4.1) is disclosed in the text and quantified in the supplement. The amendment records and per-phase reports are deposited with the journal as supplementary material.

### 7.2 What the uncertainty set does and does not cover

U reassigns molecules among a fixed cell inventory under capacity constraints, with boundary-local support and bounded leakage. It does not cover: cell merges and splits (measured separately via Proseg in E2/E3), cell-type label error (frozen; sensitivity reported), region-boundary effects (regions are assignment-independent by construction), or platform-level artifacts such as probe off-target binding (documented for Xenium panels by Bilous et al., 2026) and contextualized by the Xenium best-practice workflows of Salas et al. (2025). The certificates are therefore statements of the form: no reassignment of boundary molecules consistent with the registered constraints can change this sign. A reader who accepts the constraints accepts the sign; a reader who does not can widen U with our machinery, and the kernel margins (E4) tell them exactly how much widening each certificate tolerates.

The lung crop design deserves transparent treatment. Full-section Proseg was arithmetically unviable on our hardware; rather than tune down the science silently, we froze an expression-blind crop rule (density-median window selection with zero expression access), envelope-gated the run, and recorded the decision before launch. The crop analysis is a pre-registered subsample of the section, not the full section; the paper's lung claims are crop claims, and the full-section replication is future work enabled by nothing more than RAM.

### 7.3 The ESR1–PGR story, stated carefully

The estrogen-receptor axis produced the study's most instructive contrast, and we describe it with precision because it would be easy to overstate. In breast, ESR1↔PGR certifies negative in all three regions, but the region-0 interval is wide (width 1.23) and the Proseg point estimate for region 0 escapes the ceiling by 0.026 log2 units; under Proseg's own null, the negative direction does not reach significance in any region (q_neg = 1.0 in all three). In lung, the same pair certifies negative in all three regions with intervals of width 0.11–0.20, the Proseg estimates land inside every interval (T ≈ −1.95 to −1.96), and the Proseg-side null confirms the negative direction at q ≤ 0.027. Biologically, ESR1 and PGR are canonical co-expressed luminal markers in breast cancer (Perou et al., 2000), so a negative co-expression product there reflects the decile-matched permutation structure rather than mutual exclusion of proteins; in lung tumor tissue, where the panel measures both genes across a mixed epithelial population, the near-point-identified exclusion is the certificate that survives every validation layer applied in this study. The correct summary is: the same statistic, the same machinery, two tissues: a fragile, partially-replicating negative in breast and a robust, fully-replicating negative in lung. The framework is what makes the difference visible and quantified.

### 7.4 Limitations

1. **Single section per tissue.** Both tissues are single public sections; biological replication across patients is not addressed. The certificates are per-section statements.
2. **Labels frozen.** Cell-type label uncertainty is outside U. The cross-fitting discipline (tested genes excluded from markers) prevents the worst circularity, but label error is a real axis of uncertainty that this paper measures only via the label-transfer cross-validation and the lung marker dictionary's assertion-guarded construction.
3. **The certified statistic is a co-expression product.** It is the field's workhorse but not a measurement of physical interaction. Certificates for proximity, neighborhood, or ligand-activity statistics are future work; the separability argument extends to any statistic that is a sum of per-gene terms, and the per-gene decomposition extends to any statistic with per-gene interval bounds.
4. **One independent segmentation algorithm.** Proseg is the only re-segmentation engine we ran. The E2/E3 gates are designed to accept additional engines without modification; a third platform leg (CosMx or MERSCOPE public sections) is unblocked but not executed here.
5. **Permutation resolution.** At the registered screen resolution B = 1000, p-values floor at 9.99 × 10^-4; several headline rows sit at the floor. A pre-registered rerun at B = 10,000 with nested per-permutation seeds (the first 1,000 permutations bitwise identical to the screen, verified by an exactness gate with max |Δ| = 0.0 on both tissues) replicates both verdict sets exactly (breast 37 positive, 9 negative; lung 1 positive, 6 negative; zero flips of 129 rows) and resolves headline p-values to ≤ 9.999 × 10^-5.
6. **ε and kernel calibration.** The pseudocount choice shapes the certified set (Section 6.4), and the leakage kernel is the geometric worst case rather than an evidence-calibrated one. Both are frozen pre-analysis choices, disclosed with sensitivity, and both are targets for the calibration-upside program the margins quantify.
7. **Compute feasibility shaped the lung design.** The crop is a principled subsample but a subsample; the full-section certificate table awaits hardware or a memory-feasible reimplementation of Proseg's per-molecule state.

### 7.5 Outlook

Three extensions follow directly. First, evidence-calibrated kernels: replacing the worst-case kernel with SPARKLE-style constraints would move most currently non-identifiable rows to certified (72% on breast, 74% on lung at c = 0.5), and the margin table prices that trade per row. Second, richer statistics: neighborhood enrichment and co-localization contrasts satisfy the same separability structure, so the same certificates apply. Third, certification as review infrastructure: a journal could require, for spatial claims, an interval over a declared uncertainty set in place of a point estimate alone; the compute cost here was minutes per tissue after segmentation, and the code path is already two-tissue general.
