# Certified ligand–receptor statistics over segmentation uncertainty in spatial transcriptomics

**Running title:** Certificates for spatial segmentation uncertainty

**Abstract**

Single-molecule spatial transcriptomics resolves tissue architecture at subcellular scale, but every downstream quantity that depends on which cell owns which molecule inherits the ambiguity of cell segmentation. We ask what every reader of a cell–cell communication claim asks, and no current method answers: would the claimed interaction survive a different defensible segmentation? On a public Xenium breast-cancer section, 19 of 60 ligand–receptor contrasts pre-selected as segmentation-fragile collapse to non-identifiable under certified worst-case bounds over an explicit uncertainty set of molecule-to-cell assignments, including pairs conventional tests call strong interactions. The certificates expose a cross-tissue asymmetry on the estrogen-receptor axis: in lung tissue, ESR1–PGR mutual exclusion is certified in all three regions and replicates under an independent segmentation algorithm, while in breast the same statistic is the fragile configuration whose cross-segmentation significance fails. One lung autocrine certificate (CDH1–EGFR) mirrors breast's CDH1–ERBB2: an epithelial-adhesion-to-growth-factor coupling concordant across two tumors, panels, and instrument generations. Certificates come from certified per-gene bounds, attained where capacity constraints do not bind and certified supersets where they do, over an uncertainty set generated by boundary-local reassignment, mask erosion and dilation, and per-gene leakage intervals, confirmed by a region- and density-preserving permutation null matched to the worst-case layer (Benjamini–Hochberg FDR within region). An independent re-derivation of all 129 intervals leaves every certified verdict unchanged. The framework converts segmentation sensitivity into a per-claim error bar, flags its own marginal rows by name, and runs in minutes.

**Keywords:** spatial transcriptomics, segmentation uncertainty, robust statistics, cell–cell communication, permutation inference

---

## Introduction

Imaging-based spatial transcriptomics platforms now measure the position of individual RNA molecules in tissue sections with sub-micron accuracy. Between the measurement and any statement about cells sits an inferential step that is usually passed over in silence: segmentation, the decision of which cell each molecule belongs to. The decision is not mechanical. Molecules sit on boundaries between cells; cells touch and overlap in three dimensions; nuclear-restricted and cytoplasmic transcripts behave differently; and plausible algorithms (vendor pipelines, probabilistic simulation such as Proseg (Jones et al., 2025), admixture corrections such as cellAdmix (Mitchel et al., 2026)) disagree with one another at exactly the interfaces where biology is most interesting.

The consequences are not cosmetic. Mitchel et al. (2026) showed that segmentation errors admix molecules between adjacent cells and can dominate the error budget of downstream differential-expression and interaction analyses. Bilous et al. (2026) documented platform-level sensitivity, specificity, and signal-contamination trade-offs in Xenium data. A recent review (Ishaque et al., 2026) argues that segmentation should be treated as a central unresolved problem of spatial omics rather than a preprocessing step. In response, a family of correction methods has emerged: cellAdmix reassigns counts by matrix factorization; SPARKLE (Wang et al., 2026, preprint) corrects local RNA leakage under evidence constraints; FastReseg (Wu et al., 2025) refines masks using transcript likelihoods. All of these produce a corrected point assignment or a corrected count matrix. A corrected point is still a point. Downstream conclusions are then as fragile as the correction is lucky, and the reader has no way to know which conclusions are fragile.

The same gap runs through cell–cell communication inference. Co-expression product scores for ligand–receptor pairs underlie a large family of tools, including CellChat (Jin et al., 2021), CellPhoneDB (Efremova et al., 2020), and the LIANA benchmark comparison (Dimitrov et al., 2022), with receiver-side ligand-activity modeling in NicheNet (Browaeys et al., 2020). These methods inherit whatever assignment feeds them; none propagates assignment uncertainty into the interaction call. Computing worst-case values of a function over an uncertainty set is the paradigm of robust optimization (Ben-Tal et al., 2009). To our knowledge it has not previously been instantiated for molecule-to-cell assignment uncertainty in spatial transcriptomics, which is the gap this paper fills: the nearest objects in the literature are point corrections and posterior segmentations, none of which outputs a certified interval, a coverage-calibrated uncertainty set, or a matched null.

This paper takes a different stance. Rather than producing a better point estimate, we compute, for each conclusion, the range of values consistent with every assignment in an explicitly constructed uncertainty set, and we report a biological direction only when the entire range supports it. The output is a certificate: a claim of the form "the ligand–receptor contrast for pair p in region r lies in [T_lo, T_hi] under every assignment in U, and T_lo > 0", together with a permutation null matched to the certified object and a false-discovery-rate control over the family of tests. When the interval contains zero we do not report a trend; we report non-identifiability, which is itself informative: it says the data, at this resolution, cannot distinguish the claimed interaction from its absence under defensible segmentation choices.

Three properties distinguish the approach. First, the bounds are computed, not simulated: the certified statistic separates across genes, so worst-case optimization decomposes into per-gene problems whose optima are attained by integral assignments wherever capacity constraints do not bind (Ahuja et al., 1993); where they bind, the reported interval is a certified superset. We verify the construction by 1,000 randomized feasible configurations per gene and region (zero violations across 150,000 breast and 108,000 lung gene-level configurations), exhaustive enumeration of a worst-case subcube, bitwise agreement between the interval layers of the certify and null code paths at every null startup, and an independent re-derivation of all 129 intervals that leaves every certified verdict unchanged. Second, the null is matched to the layer of the statistic: because testing a worst-case lower bound against a null of point statistics would be trivially conservative, our permutation scheme recomputes the same worst-case objects under each region- and density-preserving label permutation. Third, the uncertainty set, the pair list, the null, and the pass/fail validation criteria were fixed in written pre-registrations before any expression computation, and all pre-registration documents, code, and hashed artifacts ship with the paper.

We apply the framework to 25 pre-specified ligand–receptor pairs across three data-driven regions of a public Xenium FFPE breast-cancer section, and then replicate the entire pipeline on an independent FFPE lung-cancer section measured with the 5,000-gene Xenium Prime panel. The two tissues differ in panel size by a factor of 16, in chemistry generation, and in tumor biology; the machinery, the validation criteria, and the statistical conventions are identical by construction. The results demonstrate both the promise and the discipline of certification: strong conclusions survive, fragile ones are named, and the one cross-platform excursion is reported with its mechanism rather than silently absorbed.

## Results

### Nineteen of sixty fragile contrasts collapse under certification

We analyzed the public Xenium v1.0.1 FFPE Human Breast Cancer Rep1 section (34.49 million QV≥20 transcripts, 167,780 vendor cells, 313-plex panel; Janesick et al., 2023). After label assignment, region definition, and band computation (65.2% of QV≥20 transcripts lie within 3 μm of a cell boundary, the dense-tumor geometry that makes assignment ambiguity unavoidable), we computed certified intervals for 25 pairs × 3 regions. Twenty of the 25 pairs were selected before analysis as segmentation-fragile, on biological class and interface exposure and never on effect size; five pairs whose reliability is expected from the literature served as positive controls.

Of 75 rows, 37 are certified positive and 9 certified negative under the matched null; 29 are non-identifiable (Figure 1). Among the 60 rows selected as fragile, 19 collapse to non-identifiable and 6 certify negative, including the estrogen-receptor axis ESR1↔PGR, whose certified-negative status in breast tissue is itself biologically coherent (the co-expression product of two luminal markers sits below its permutation null when labels are decile-matched; see below). All five positive controls certify positive in at least one region (7 of 15 control rows), satisfying the pre-specified criterion that at least one positive control certify. Two controls do not certify positive in any region: CCL5→CCR7, a low-count chemokine pair, and CD274→PDCD1, whose certified-negative rows reflect the expected trans-cellular disco-localization of a membrane ligand and were declared non-gating at pre-registration. The count is reported as observed.

**Figure 1.** Certified intervals for all 25 pairs in the breast section. Each row shows the certified interval [T_lo, T_hi] (bar) and the vendor point estimate T(A0) (dot) for three data-driven regions (offset vertically within each pair). Colors encode the verdict under the matched permutation null (q ≤ 0.10): certified positive (red), certified negative (blue), non-identifiable (grey). Controls are prefixed "CTRL" and bolded. The dashed vertical line marks T = 0.

The interval widths carry the mechanistic story. Certified-positive rows have median width 3.34 log2 units (interquartile range 2.40–4.58): the tumor-epithelial pairs (ACTA2→ERBB2, KRT8→ERBB2, CDH1→ERBB2) certify with lower bounds far from zero, while interface pairs such as CD68→CD274 certify with lower bounds barely above zero (+0.07 in region 0). These are exactly the rows a strict reader should trust least, and exactly the rows the cross-segmentation test below interrogates.

### Certification and significance are complementary, and neither suffices alone

The matched null changes conclusions in both directions. Five rows whose intervals exclude zero at the interval layer fail the matched null: their worst-case bound is not extreme relative to worst-case objects under density-matched label permutations, so they are not certified. Conversely, the worst-case layer protects against the opposite error. Nineteen fragile rows carry point estimates as large as +2.24 log2 units, values a conventional analysis would report as strong interactions, yet their certified intervals contain zero, because defensible reassignment of boundary molecules can drive the contrast through zero. Neither layer alone is sufficient; the certificate is the conjunction. We regard the five dropped rows as the framework refusing to claim more than it can prove.

Three examples make the value of the format concrete. First, ERBB2→EGFR in region 0 has a point estimate far from zero (+3.71) and a certified interval of [+1.55, +4.58], yet it fails the matched null in that region (q_pos = 1.0) and is reported as non-identifiable. Second, ESR1→PGR in region 0 has a modest point estimate (−0.71) yet certifies negative (T_hi = −0.27), because the interval machinery accounts for every reassignment that could push the co-expression product upward and finds none sufficient. Third, the lung CDH1→EGFR certificate (below) holds at T_lo = +0.21 despite a point estimate of +3.39. In each case the certificate carries information the point estimate cannot.

### Proseg confirms the geometry of the certified intervals

Proseg (v3.2.0) re-segmented the identical molecule file into 165,094 cells. Because Proseg merges and splits cells, its assignment is not a member of U; it serves as an independent probe of interval calibration. Under the frozen label-transfer protocol (nearest-centroid in marker space, tested genes excluded, two-fold cross-validated at 72.4% overall and 85–97% on analysis-critical types), 72 of 75 rows (96.0%) fall inside their certified intervals, above the pre-registered 90% criterion. Sign agreement on all 46 certified rows is 46/46. The three excursions are all region-0 rows (ERBB2→PDCD1, ESR1→PGR, PGR→ESR1) with magnitudes of 0.03–0.05 log2 units, all preserving the certified sign.

The Proseg-side matched null asks a sharper question: are the certified rows significant under the independent segmentation's own null? Forty of 46 certified rows (87.0%) pass at q ≤ 0.10, above the 85% criterion. All 37 certified-positive rows replicate; the six failures are the ESR1↔PGR axis in all three regions, a single biological configuration: Proseg's assignment places ESR1 and PGR in the same luminal population such that the co-expression product is less negative than density-matched label permutations typically produce (point estimates remain negative and inside their certified intervals in regions 1 and 2; region 0 sits 0.026 log2 units above the ceiling). The vendor certificate is not invalidated, but the cross-segmentation significance of the negative direction for this configuration does not replicate, and we report it as a finding with its mechanism.

### Certificates quantify their own margin against leakage miscalibration

For each certified row we computed the critical kernel multiplier c*: the largest factor by which the leakage flow could inflate beyond the boundary-local worst case before the interval ceases to exclude zero. The median certified breast row survives a 1.28× inflation of boundary leakage; 69.6% survive 1.25×, 15.2% survive 2×, and none is immune to unbounded inflation, as expected for interval certificates over a flow family. Positive controls are the most robust (median c* 1.49 versus 1.28 for fragile pairs; Figure 2). The margins also quantify the value of better leakage calibration: with a kernel twice as conservative as the geometric worst case (c = 0.5, the regime an evidence-calibrated kernel occupies), 21 of 29 non-certified breast rows would certify, including the five matched-null drops. Calibration, not computation, is the binding constraint on certification power.

Each point is one certificate (jittered); black bars mark medians, gray bands interquartile ranges; log scale; dotted line marks the registered boundary-local kernel (c = 1). Lung certificates tolerate a median 20.4-fold kernel inflation versus 1.28-fold on breast, reflecting the tight lung intervals produced by the 5K panel's per-cell counts.

Sensitivity analyses support the certified set. At the interval layer, ε = 0.1 yields 19 interval-positive and 20 interval-negative breast rows (versus 39/12 at ε = 0.5), and ε = 1.0 yields 75/0; the direction is mechanical, since larger ε compresses all log-ratios toward zero from above, and ε enters observed intervals and the null identically. The pre-registered narrower band (d = 2 μm) leaves every observed statistic inside its certified interval (75/75 breast). As an outside-U perturbation, nucleus-only assignment (which deletes cytoplasmic molecules wholesale) pushes 7 of 75 breast rows outside their certified intervals, all involving stromal or myeloid senders (CXCL12, CD68) whose cytoplasmic signal the nucleus assignment deletes: the expected signature of a deliberately out-of-set perturbation, reported rather than gated. The full tables are in the Supplement.

### The lung replication: one positive and six negative certificates on an independent tissue and panel

We then replicated the entire pipeline, unchanged, on the Xenium Prime 5K FFPE Human Lung Cancer section (149.4 million QV≥20 transcripts over 71.1 mm², 5,001-gene panel): 4.3× the molecule count of breast at 16× the panel size. The replication was pre-registered end-to-end, including dataset identity, audit gates, and compute plan, before any lung statistic was computed. Two audit findings reshaped the design, both mechanically per the frozen gates. Four tested genes (ACTA2, CCL5, CD3D, KRT8) are absent from the 5K panel, leaving 18 of 25 pairs testable, with the 7 exclusions recorded; no pair was renamed or substituted. And because Proseg's memory scales with molecule state (~230 bytes/transcript), full-section lung segmentation requires ~25–35 GB against 15.7 GB of physical RAM and was arithmetically unviable; the registered fallback was an expression-blind spatial crop, a 3.5 × 3.5 mm window (47,754 vendor cells, 25.55 million transcripts) selected by median vendor-cell density over a 0.5 mm grid with zero access to expression data, envelope-gated at a predicted 6.90 GB peak before launch. The crop's vendor assignment layer reproduces the vendor matrix at per-cell concordance 0.9568 (total ratio 0.9694, at the crop's edge-clipping boundary). Here the capacity constraint of the uncertainty set binds for 197 cells, so the certified bounds are tightened by the one sound capped formula used everywhere (Methods); lung intervals are certified supersets.

Of 54 rows, one is certified positive and six certified negative (Figure 3):

- **CDH1→EGFR, region 0: T ∈ [+0.21, +3.72], q_pos < 0.001.** A tumor-epithelial coupling between the junction protein E-cadherin's transcript and EGFR, the lung analogue of breast's certified CDH1→ERBB2. The concordance of the epithelial-adhesion-to-growth-factor axis across two independent tumors, panels, and instrument generations is the kind of convergence the certificate format makes visible.
- **ESR1↔PGR mutual exclusion in all three regions** (T_hi ∈ [−1.88, −1.79], matched q_neg ≤ 0.027 under the add-one convention). The same pair that is the fragile configuration in breast is here the strongest negative certificate in the study, with intervals so tight (median certified-row width 0.18 log2 units) that the exclusion is nearly point-identified.

Cross-segmentation results mirror breast's structure. Proseg re-segmented the crop into 47,286 cells (0.98% fewer than vendor). Coverage: 6 of 7 certified rows fall inside their certified intervals (85.7%, below the 90% criterion and above the 75% floor at which the design declares failure). The single excursion is CDH1→EGFR region 0 itself: T_proseg = +4.04 against a ceiling of +3.72, +0.32 log2 units above the ceiling, sign preserved, and significant under the Proseg-side null in the certified direction (q = 0.0011). This is the same magnitude-excursion class breast showed (three excursions of 0.03–0.05 log2 units, all sign-preserving), and the one row whose point estimate outruns its certificate is named with its mechanism. Significance under the Proseg-side null: 7 of 7 certified rows (100%), including the excursion row. Kernel margins are markedly higher on lung (median c* = 20.4; 6 of 7 rows survive a 3× inflation; the mutual-exclusion certificates tolerate 16–36×), driven by the tight intervals that the 5K panel's per-cell counts produce; with a twice-conservative kernel (c = 0.5), 35 of 47 non-certified lung rows would certify.

### ESR1–PGR: one statistic, two tissues, two behaviors

The estrogen-receptor axis produced the study's most instructive contrast, and we describe it precisely because it would be easy to overstate. In breast, ESR1↔PGR certifies negative in all three regions, but the region-0 interval is wide (width 1.23) and the Proseg point estimate for region 0 escapes the ceiling by 0.026 log2 units; under Proseg's own null the negative direction does not reach significance in any region (q_neg = 1.0 in all three). In lung, the same pair certifies negative in all three regions with intervals of width 0.11–0.20, the Proseg estimates land inside every interval (T ≈ −1.95 to −1.96), and the Proseg-side null confirms the negative direction at q ≤ 0.027. Biologically, ESR1 and PGR are canonical co-expressed luminal markers in breast cancer (Perou et al., 2000), so a negative co-expression product there reflects the decile-matched permutation structure rather than mutual exclusion of proteins; in lung tumor tissue, where the panel measures both genes across a mixed epithelial population, the near-point-identified exclusion is the certificate that survives every validation layer applied in this study. The correct summary is the same statistic and the same machinery in two tissues: a fragile, partially-replicating negative in breast and a robust, fully-replicating negative in lung. The framework is what makes the difference visible and quantified.

At the registered screen resolution of B = 1,000 permutations, p-values floor at 9.99 × 10⁻⁴ and several headline rows sit at the floor. A pre-registered rerun at B = 10,000 with nested per-permutation seeds (the first 1,000 permutations bitwise identical to the screen, verified by an exactness gate with maximum absolute difference 0.0 on both tissues) replicates both verdict sets exactly (breast 37 positive, 9 negative; lung 1 positive, 6 negative; zero flips of 129 rows) and resolves headline p-values to ≤ 9.999 × 10⁻⁵.

## Discussion

### Certification as a deliverable

The spatial-omics community has converged on a real problem, the contamination of cell-level conclusions by segmentation uncertainty, and on a family of point solutions. This paper changes the deliverable. Instead of a corrected matrix, the analyst receives per-claim certificates: an interval valid over an explicit, reproducible uncertainty set, a null matched to the certified object, and a family-wise error rate. Non-identifiability becomes a first-class output. The format changes behavior at the moment of reading: a table entry either carries a certificate or carries an honest admission that the platform, at this resolution, cannot support the claim.

### What the uncertainty set does and does not cover

U reassigns molecules among a fixed cell inventory under capacity constraints, with boundary-local support and bounded leakage. It does not cover cell merges and splits (measured separately via Proseg), cell-type label error (labels are frozen, with every tested gene excluded from the marker panel to prevent circularity), region-boundary effects (regions are assignment-independent by construction), or platform-level artifacts such as probe off-target binding (documented for Xenium panels by Bilous et al., 2026 and contextualized by Salas et al., 2025). The certificates are therefore statements of the form: no reassignment of boundary molecules consistent with the registered constraints can change this sign. A reader who accepts the constraints accepts the sign; a reader who does not can widen U with our machinery, and the kernel margins tell them exactly how much widening each certificate tolerates.

The lung analysis is a pre-registered expression-blind subsample of the section rather than the full section, because full-section segmentation was arithmetically unviable on our hardware; the crop rule was frozen, with zero expression access, before any lung statistic was computed. The paper's lung claims are crop claims, and we state them as such.

### Scope and limitations

Five limitations bound the claims. Both tissues are single public sections, so biological replication across patients is not addressed and the certificates are per-section statements. Cell-type label uncertainty is outside U; the cross-fitting discipline prevents the worst circularity, but label error remains a real axis measured only indirectly. The certified statistic is the field's workhorse co-expression product, not a measurement of physical interaction; the separability argument extends to any statistic that is a sum of per-gene terms, and neighborhood and proximity statistics are natural next targets. Proseg is the only independent re-segmentation engine we ran; the validation criteria accept additional engines without modification. Finally, the leakage kernel is the geometric worst case rather than an evidence-calibrated one; both the kernel and the pseudocount are frozen pre-analysis choices disclosed with sensitivity.

### Outlook

Three extensions follow directly. Evidence-calibrated kernels, replacing the worst-case kernel with SPARKLE-style constraints, would move most currently non-identifiable rows to certified (72% on breast, 74% on lung at c = 0.5), and the margin table prices that trade per row. Richer statistics, neighborhood enrichment and co-localization contrasts among them, satisfy the same separability structure, so the same certificates apply. And certification could serve as review infrastructure: a journal could require, for spatial claims, an interval over a declared uncertainty set in place of a point estimate alone. The compute cost here was minutes per tissue after segmentation, and the code path is already two-tissue general.

## Methods

### Data and preprocessing

We analyzed two public 10x Genomics Xenium sections: Xenium FFPE Human Breast Cancer Rep1 (Xenium v1.0.1, 313-plex panel; 42,638,083 raw transcripts, 34,493,510 QV≥20, 167,780 vendor cells) and Xenium Prime 5K FFPE Human Lung Cancer (5,001-gene panel; 149.4 million QV≥20 transcripts over a 71.09 mm² section). All analysis used QV≥20 transcripts. The vendor assignment was reproduced from molecules and polygon geometry by point-in-polygon classification and verified against the vendor cell-by-gene matrix before any statistic was computed: 91.5% of cells exactly on breast (per-gene Pearson r median 0.991) and per-cell concordance 0.9146 (total ratio 0.9736); on lung, per-cell concordance 0.9568 (total ratio 0.9694). The discrepancy concentrates at shared boundaries and in the vendor's documented nucleus-expansion heuristics; we compute all certificates over our own polygon-interior assignment A0 consistently, and we regard the 2–4% boundary-class disagreement between two readings of the same vendor output as direct evidence that "the" assignment is one choice among defensible ones, which is the paper's thesis made concrete.

Regions are k-means (k = 3) clusters of cells over (log10(1+transcript counts), x, y), computed once from A0 and frozen; they are assignment-independent by construction and held fixed across all analyses, with Proseg cells inheriting the region of the nearest vendor centroid. Cell-type labels were assigned once by majority vote over marker-panel annotations, with every gene appearing in a tested pair excluded from the marker panel (cross-fitting against the tested statistics). On breast, the panel's own annotation column provides the marker lists; on lung, where no per-gene annotation file could be obtained, a frozen literature marker dictionary plays the same role, with tested-gene leakage caught by assertion and filtered mechanically.

### The uncertainty set

U is generated by three mechanisms, all fixed in pre-registration. (1) Boundary reassignment: any transcript within distance d = 3 μm of a cell boundary (the "band") may be kept in its vendor cell, moved to any cell whose boundary edge lies within 2d = 6 μm of the transcript (its donor set), or dropped to extracellular; band membership and donor sets are computed from polygon geometry only. (2) Mask erosion and dilation: global erosion of all cell masks by 2 μm removes band transcripts from all cells, and dilation absorbs extracellular band transcripts into the nearest cell; both are special cases of the flow family. (3) Leakage kernel: a per-gene fraction λ_g ∈ [λ0_g, λ1_g] of a cell's transcripts may be treated as leaked, calibrated from cytoplasmic/nuclear ratios of frozen calibration genes. Plausibility constraints: a cell may gain at most 50% and lose at most 60% of its original transcript count through reassignment; labels are frozen; region membership is assignment-independent. U varies the assignment of molecules among a fixed cell inventory; it does not include segmentation schemes that merge or split cells (that sensitivity is measured through Proseg) nor label uncertainty (reported as sensitivity).

### Certified bounds

For a ligand–receptor pair (L, R) with sender type S and receiver type R′, let E_s(g) be the mean number of transcripts of gene g per cell of type s in region ρ, restricted to cells with at least ten total transcripts, and

  T(A) = log2( (E_S(L; A) + ε) (E_{R′}(R; A) + ε) ),  ε = 0.5,

the log of the product of per-cell ligand and receptor abundances, the standard co-expression form (Jin et al., 2021; Efremova et al., 2020). The pseudocount was frozen before any computation. A row is certified positive if T_lo > 0 and certified negative if T_hi < 0, where [T_lo, T_hi] = [min_{A∈U} T(A), max_{A∈U} T(A)]; certification additionally requires matched-null significance in the certified direction.

T(A) = f(N_L(A)) + g(N_R(A)) with f(n) = log2(n/n_L + ε) and g(n) = log2(n/n_R + ε); the two terms depend on disjoint transcript sets, so the extremes are attained simultaneously and T_lo = f(min_A N_L) + g(min_A N_R), T_hi = f(max_A N_L) + g(max_A N_R). For a single gene, the achievable values of N_L over U form a contiguous integer interval: the max-flow problem on the bipartite graph (movable transcripts → destinations) with per-cell vertex capacities has integral optimal solutions, and flow decomposition attains every intermediate value (Ahuja et al., 1993). We compute the extremes by an aggregate per-gene construction: each movable transcript contributes an independent delta given its own donor slots, and the per-cell gain capacities, which feasibility checks prove non-binding on breast, make that aggregate optimum attained. Where capacities bind (197 of 47,754 lung-crop cells, including 41 zero-count vendor artifact cells; never on breast), we tighten the upper bound by the per-cell separable argument dmax = min(dmax_uncapped, Σ_c min(potential_c, cap_c)), sound because each gain into cell c consumes a distinct transcript listing c and respects the capacity; the same formula runs in the certify step, the permutation null, and the observed-value cross-check. Lung intervals are therefore certified supersets: provably valid, marginally wider.

Verification is four-way. First, exhaustive enumeration of a worst-case subcube confirms the construction's extremes. Second, 1,000 randomized feasible configurations per gene and region produce zero violations across 150,000 breast and 108,000 lung gene-level configurations. Third, the certify-path and null-path interval layers agree bitwise at every null startup (all 75 breast and 54 lung rows within 1e-6). Fourth, an independent re-derivation of all 129 intervals reproduces every certified verdict, tightens median widths by 9.2% (breast) and 14.2% (lung), and shows the originally vectorized construction was uniformly conservative on the gains side, because its segment aggregation absorbed interleaved other-gene donor slots; because the null recomputed the same worst-case objects under the same rule, the matched test was internally fair and every p-value stands. The primary analysis reports the pre-registered construction, whose every interval is a certified superset; the corrected rule certifies strictly more (three additional interval-negative rows, Supplement S8) and is deposited alongside it as verification, so a reader recomputing the bounds finds tighter intervals and the same verdicts.

### Matched permutation null

Testing T_lo against a null distribution of point statistics would be a category error: every permutation's worst-case minimum is trivially at most its point value. Our null therefore recomputes the same worst-case objects under each permutation: for permutation b with permuted labels, T_lo⁽ᵇ⁾ and T_hi⁽ᵇ⁾ are computed by the identical interval machinery as the observed bounds, because permutation preserves donor geometry (movable sets and donor adjacencies are label-independent). Labels are permuted within (region × transcript-count-decile) blocks, preserving region composition and count structure. We use B = 1,000 permutations with per-permutation documented seeds, p_pos = (1 + #{T_lo⁽ᵇ⁾ ≥ T_lo_obs})/(B+1) and p_neg = (1 + #{T_hi⁽ᵇ⁾ ≤ T_hi_obs})/(B+1) (Phipson and Smyth, 2010; North et al., 2002), and Benjamini–Hochberg control within region over the 25 (breast) or 18 (lung) pairs at q ≤ 0.10 (Benjamini and Hochberg, 1995). A rerun at B = 10,000 used nested seeds so that the first 1,000 permutations are bitwise identical to the screen (verified by an exactness gate); the registered verdicts were unchanged on both tissues. The null itself was a pre-registered correction: the originally registered point-layer null was identified as mismatched before any inference was drawn, the correction was registered before computing, and the invalid artifact was quarantined rather than deleted.

### Sensitivity analyses

Pseudocount sensitivity recomputes the interval layer at ε ∈ {0.1, 0.5, 1.0}; ε enters observed intervals and the null identically, so comparisons within a fixed ε are internally consistent. Band-width sensitivity at d = 2 μm shrinks U monotonically. The nucleus-only assignment is a deliberately out-of-set perturbation and is reported as such. Kernel margins: for each certified row, c* is the largest factor by which the leakage flow could inflate beyond the boundary-local worst case before the interval ceases to exclude zero, computed by bisection on a provably monotone family of intervals, exact to |T| ≤ 1e-10 at the root, with direction-aware sign-transition verification on every row. c = 1 is the registered kernel.

### Validation criteria and cross-segmentation protocol

The tested-pair set is the subset of 25 pre-specified pairs whose both genes exist in the panel; exclusions are mechanical and recorded with the missing gene. Pre-specified criteria: at least one positive control certified while at least one fragile pair loses sign certification; cross-segmentation coverage (fraction of certified rows whose T under Proseg falls inside its certified interval) ≥ 90% passes and 75–90% is reported as partial with mandatory mechanistic diagnosis; cross-segmentation significance (fraction of certified rows significant under Proseg's own matched null in the certified direction) ≥ 85% passes and 60–85% is partial. All criteria, including the interpretation of failures as findings to be reported with mechanism, were fixed before any expression computation. Proseg 3.2.0 was run on the identical molecule file with a memory-feasible configuration (2 μm voxels, 2 z-layers; 165,094 cells breast; 47,286 cells lung crop), and labels were transferred by nearest-centroid in marker space with tested genes excluded.

## Data and code availability

All data are public: Xenium FFPE Human Breast Cancer Rep1 and Xenium Prime 5K FFPE Human Lung Cancer (10x Genomics), with exact CDN URLs and byte sizes recorded in the repository pre-registrations. Code, pre-registrations, the theory document, and SHA-256 manifests covering every result artifact are available at https://github.com/amywang2010/b03-certificates. Every figure and every number in the text traces to a hashed CSV or JSON artifact. The certificate computation requires Python 3.12+ (numpy, pandas, pyarrow) and runs in minutes per tissue on a workstation (8 cores, 15.7 GB RAM); the permutation nulls checkpoint and resume exactly.
